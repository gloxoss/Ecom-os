- **Scope**: Catalog copy, technical specifications, and manufacturer assertions

---

## 1. Status correction, 2026-10-11

The first revision of this inventory marked six claims **APPROVED** with an
`Evidence Source` column that contained only a *description* of the document that
would support the claim ("Fiche technique fabricant", "Validation clinique ESH").
No source document, model number, certificate identifier or manufacturer
attestation was attached, and no product-specific model was named. An approval
recorded against a description is not an approval.

Those six verdicts are therefore withdrawn and set to **PENDING OWNER EVIDENCE**.
This is not a finding that any claim is false. It is a finding that nothing here
substantiates it, so none of it may support a release certification.

The audit's requirement (finding 8): product-specific claim sources and an
attributable approval record. Until an owner supplies them, these claims must
remain visibly pending.

## 3. What is established

The compliance *policy* is genuine and conservative: no invented registration
numbers, CE marks or FDA certifications; no clinical superiority claims; warranty
statements limited to manufacturer terms; mandatory patient disclaimers on
diagnostic, orthopaedic and mobility product pages. CLM-07 and CLM-08 remain
unpublished pending signed documentation.

What was missing is the evidence for the individual product claims, not the
intent to avoid unsupported claims.

---

## 4. Required to close

For each of CLM-01 through CLM-06, the owner supplies the named document and the
exact product model it covers. A reviewer then records: the claim, the source
document identifier, the model, the reviewer, and the date. Only then does a
verdict other than PENDING become available.

Owner-supplied facts that also block release are tracked in
[clients/paraloca/content-approval.md](../../../../clients/paraloca/content-approval.md).
