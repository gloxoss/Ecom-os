Everything you need to know about our coffee

Do you include roast dates?

Yes, every package includes the roast date so you know exactly how fresh your coffee is.

What makes your coffee special?

We focus on quality at every step—from ethically sourced beans to precise brewing techniques. Every cup is crafted to highlight natural flavors and deliver a consistently rich experience.

Can I subscribe or order coffee regularly?

Yes! We offer subscription plans to deliver your favorite coffees right to your door, so you never run out of fresh beans.

Do you offer decaf options?

Absolutely! Our decaf coffees retain full flavor while minimizing caffeine, perfect for evenings or anyone avoiding caffeine.

How do I brew the best cup at home?

Use freshly ground beans, clean equipment, and the right water temperature (195–205°F / 90–96°C). Adjust grind and brew time to taste.

What’s the difference between single origin and blends?

Single origin coffee comes from one region, highlighting unique flavors, while blends combine beans from multiple regions for a balanced, consistent taste.
