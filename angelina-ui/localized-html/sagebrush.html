Description

Sagebrush Decaf Coffee is a smooth and comforting blend designed for those who love rich coffee flavor without the caffeine. Made from carefully selected Arabica beans, this coffee is roasted to bring out warm, nutty aromas and a naturally balanced sweetness. Each cup reveals gentle notes of toasted hazelnut and soft cocoa, creating a mellow and satisfying taste with a silky body.

The medium roast enhances the coffee’s smooth character while keeping the flavor clean and well-rounded. With its low acidity and subtle sweetness, Sagebrush Decaf is perfect for relaxed mornings, evening coffee breaks, or any moment when you want a flavorful cup without the caffeine kick.

Payment & Shipping Information

We aim to make your shopping experience simple, secure, and convenient. We accept major credit and debit cards as well as popular online payment methods to ensure a smooth checkout process. All transactions are securely processed to protect your personal and payment information.

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days after confirmation. Once your order is shipped, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking details so you can follow your package. Delivery times may vary depending on your location, but most orders arrive within 3–7 business days. We carefully pack all coffee to maintain freshness and ensure it reaches you in perfect condition.

Exchanges & Returns

Your satisfaction is important to us. If there is an issue with your order, such as a damaged package or incorrect product, please contact us within 7 days of delivery and our team will be happy to assist you.

Due to the nature of coffee products, opened packages generally cannot be returned. However, if your item arrives damaged or defective, we will gladly arrange a replacement or refund. For unopened items, returns or exchanges may be accepted within 14 days of delivery, provided the product remains sealed and in its original condition.
