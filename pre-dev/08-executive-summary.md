## What we're building for [CLIENT NAME]

A modern, fast, MENA-ready online store — built on **Ecom OS**, EPIOSO's pre-built e-commerce platform. Same proven core that powers our other deployments, customized to your brand and your specific needs.

## Why this matters

You don't need another templated WooCommerce site that loads slowly and breaks every time a plugin updates. You also don't need a quarter-million-dirham custom build that takes a year. Ecom OS is the middle: **the speed and quality of a custom build, at the price and timeline of a templated one** — because we've already built 80% of it.

## How it works

1. **Universal storefront** built in modern Next.js — fast on mobile, fast on 3G, fast on the slowest Moroccan connection.
2. **Backend engine** you can choose: WooCommerce if you want the familiar WordPress admin, or our custom NestJS/Vendure setup if you need more control at scale.
3. **Built-in addons** for the things that matter regionally:
   - **Multi-language** (French + Arabic + English, with proper right-to-left for Arabic)
   - **Multi-payment** (Cash on Delivery + CMI + Stripe)
   - **Multi-branch fulfillment** routing orders to the nearest store *(available in v1.1)*
4. **Brand-able in hours**, not weeks — we configure your logo, colors, fonts, and copy directly in the platform.
