# Identity Sheet — Ecom OS visual brand (consistency pack)

Shared across ALL assets in the `ecom-os-launch` campaign. Reuse this locked base — never re-describe the brand from scratch. Source of truth: DESIGN.md (v2.0 palette) + docs/brand-guidelines.md (voice).

## Identity
Ecom OS — EPIOSO's pre-built e-commerce platform. Brand-as-subject (no character/mascot). The recurring visual protagonist is **the modular grid**: a 2×2 arrangement of rounded square tiles, three in Noir, one in Ember, the Ember tile slightly offset — a stable core with one swappable module.

## Non-negotiable visual traits
- 2×2 grid of rounded squares, corner radius = 25% of tile width
- Exactly ONE Ember tile (top-right), offset ~10% outward from the grid
- Palette locked to hex: Ember `#FA7400` · Cacao `#763B00` · Noir `#000000` · Warm White `#FFFBFA` · White `#FFFFFF` (+ derived muted `#F6EFE9`)
- Warm White (`#FFFBFA`) backgrounds on light compositions; pure Noir (`#000000`) on dark ones
- Ember ≤ 10% of any composition; Cacao ≤ 5%; the rest Warm White/White + Noir
- Typography in-image: geometric sans (Space Grotesk look) for headlines, monospace for proof numbers, proof numbers rendered in Ember
- Adjacent tiles may fuse with smooth concave metaball fillet necks (see logo-prompts.md references)

## Material / texture palette
Matte flat surfaces with very subtle paper grain; optional physical variant: matte painted ceramic / clay tiles (a quiet zellige nod) on warm-white plaster. No gloss, no chrome, no glass.

## Scene constraints
Swiss-grid alignment, generous negative space (≥40% of canvas), even studio lighting or soft directional daylight, shallow soft shadows only (no hard drop shadows).

## Forbidden variations
Gradients between brand colors · glassmorphism/blur panels · neon glows · extra or missing tiles · recolored tiles · mascots/faces/hands · laptop-and-latte stock imagery · emoji · more than one accent color per composition · bouncy/cartoonish depth · **drifting Ember `#FA7400` toward EPIOSO's `#FF6B2D` or generic red-orange**.

## Reusable base prompt (prepend to every campaign image)
> Minimal editorial brand graphic on a warm off-white background (#FFFBFA) with subtle paper grain. Central motif: a 2x2 grid of four rounded squares (corner radius 25%), three tiles in pure black (#000000), the top-right tile in vivid warm orange (#FA7400) offset slightly outward as if snapping into place. Thin deep-brown (#763B00) accents. Swiss grid layout, generous negative space, flat graphic style with faint soft shadows, even studio lighting. No gradients, no gloss, no glassmorphism.

## 5 locked variants (identity preserved)
1. **Launch hero** — base prompt + headline "The 80% is done." in geometric sans, black, lower third; mono stat line in orange (#FA7400) beneath.
2. **Physical/ceramic** — the grid as matte clay tiles (black glaze + one orange glaze) on warm white plaster, soft daylight from the left, photographic.
3. **Diagram-as-art** — grid tiles connected by thin deep-brown (#763B00) connector lines to a larger black rectangle (the storefront), architecture-diagram aesthetic.
4. **RTL/i18n angle** — mirrored composition, Arabic headline «‎80٪ جاهزة» set right-to-left, same grid motif.
5. **Proof-number poster** — giant monospace "2" in orange (#FA7400) filling the left half, grid motif small in the corner, caption "weeks to staging".
